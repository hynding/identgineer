Bottom region

Centred in the whole block

A cover is a minimum height and not a fixed one, so content that needs more room than the minimum makes it taller instead of overflowing. This paragraph is long enough to need several lines in a narrow container, and the block grows with it. The whole point of a minimum is that the content never has to fit inside it. A banner that is a screen high on a tall monitor is still a banner on a phone held upright, where the text beneath it simply keeps going. Nothing is clipped, nothing is scaled and nothing scrolls inside the block, because the block is only ever as short as its content allows and no shorter than the step it was given.
